Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Dateryx is a social media analytics project intended to help account owners review content performance using information they authorize us to access. This Privacy Policy explains the intended collection, use, storage, and deletion of information when using Dateryx and its associated analytics application, SocialStats Time Out.

Current testing scope: Dateryx is currently being prepared for testing with an account controlled by the project operator. Connections to additional customer accounts are not currently offered.

1. Information we may access

When an account owner authorizes a supported platform connection, and subject to that platform's permissions and API availability, Dateryx may access:

  • Basic account information, such as account identifiers, display names, and profile details.
  • Information about authorized videos or posts, such as identifiers, captions, and publication dates.
  • Available performance metrics, such as video views, likes, comments, and shares.
  • Access credentials or tokens necessary to maintain an authorized API connection, if a connection is enabled.

We only intend to request information needed for the analytics features being tested. Not every metric listed above will necessarily be available through a platform's API.

2. How information is used

Authorized information is intended to be used to display analytics, compare content performance, track changes over time, troubleshoot integrations, and maintain authorized connections. Dateryx does not intend to sell personal information or use connected account data for third-party advertising.

3. Storage and access

During the current testing phase, analytics data is intended to be processed by SocialStats Time Out and stored locally on the project operator's computer, including in its local SQLite database. The public Dateryx website hosted on GitHub Pages is informational and does not display the private analytics database.

Platform authorization and API communication may involve the relevant platform and its service providers. The security and storage arrangements for any future hosted or multi-user version will be described in an updated policy before those features are offered.

4. Sharing of information

Dateryx does not intend to sell or publish connected account analytics. Information may be exchanged with a connected platform as necessary to complete an authorized API request, or disclosed when legally required.

5. Data retention and deletion

During local testing, stored analytics may remain in the local application until they are deleted by the project operator. To request removal of information associated with a connected account, or to ask about revoking access, contact dateryx.oficial@gmail.com. We will review the request and explain the applicable deletion steps. Account owners may also revoke platform access through the platform's account settings where supported.

6. Security

Dateryx is intended to use official platform authorization methods and to limit access to authorized accounts. No system can guarantee absolute security. Access tokens and other credentials should not be published in the public website or source repository.

7. Children's privacy

Dateryx is not designed for children and does not intentionally seek to collect children's personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Dateryx develops, particularly if additional account connections, hosting, or customer-facing features are introduced. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For questions about privacy, connected accounts, or deletion requests, email dateryx.oficial@gmail.com.
